01 / About GODSOBSERVATÓRIO NACIONAL · RIO DE JANEIRO

Every satellite has a story to tell.

Natural satellites hold clues to the formation and evolution of planetary systems. Their orbits, internal properties and interactions reveal the physical processes that shape worlds over time.

GODS — the Group of Origin and Dynamics of Satellites — studies these systems through celestial mechanics, theoretical models and numerical investigations, connecting fundamental dynamics with planetary science.
