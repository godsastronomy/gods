01 / About GODS

The Group of Origin and Dynamics of Satellites.

GODS is a planetary science research group dedicated to understanding the origin and dynamical evolution of natural satellites and circumplanetary systems.

Our work combines numerical simulations and theoretical models to investigate accretion in giant-impact-generated disks, interactions between rings and satellites, and exomoon systems.
